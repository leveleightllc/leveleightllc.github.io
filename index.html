Level Eight LLC is a company based in Austin, Texas. We provide educational services and useful applications that allow people to manage various parts of their lives and achieve their peak potential.

What we make

Fog Log, a private daily log for symptoms and the things you take, for iPhone.

Fog Log records how you feel each day alongside whatever you are taking, so that over weeks you can see whether anything changed. It is built for people living with brain fog and fatigue, including long COVID. Everything stays on the phone. The app has no account and makes no network requests.

About the app · User guide · Support · Privacy policy

How we build

  • Private by design. Our apps keep your information on your device. We do not run servers that hold your data, we show no ads, and we sell nothing about you.
  • Useful before clever. Each feature has to save someone time or show them something they could not see before.
  • No advice we are not qualified to give. Health tools record what you tell them and show you the pattern. They never suggest what to take.
  • Your records stay yours. Everything can be exported in standard formats (PDF and spreadsheet) at any time, at no charge.
