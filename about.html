About Level Eight

Level Eight LLC is a software and education company based in Austin, Texas. We make applications that help people manage parts of their lives (their health, their habits, their time) and get to the level they are capable of.

Why we exist

Much of the software people rely on to manage their lives is built to keep them subscribed, to collect their data, or both. A medication tracker that holds years of someone’s history hostage behind a subscription is not serving that person. We think there is room for tools that do one job well, respect the people using them, and leave them in full control of their own information.

What we are working on

Our first product is Fog Log, a private symptom and medication log for iPhone, built for people living with brain fog and fatigue. It is currently in beta testing with a group of users ahead of its App Store release.

Alongside our apps, we develop educational material and guides that help people get the most from the tools they use.
